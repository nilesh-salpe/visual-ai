Post-training LLMs

How a pretrained model becomes an assistant: supervised fine-tuning, LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) and QLoRA (Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation), reward models, RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback) with PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization), reward hacking, DPO (Direct Preference Optimization) and its relatives, RL (reinforcement learning) with verifiable rewards (GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization)), and Constitutional AI (artificial intelligence). Every deck follows the 9-beat template, numbers are checked against the source papers, and most concepts have a hands-on Colab notebook.

🛠 Build it: 8 Colab notebooks

Implement SFT (supervised fine-tuning) loss masking and packing, LoRA, NF4 (4-bit NormalFloat) quantization, a Bradley-Terry reward model, PPO with a KL (Kullback-Leibler divergence) penalty, best-of-n overoptimization, DPO and GRPO in NumPy. Built-in tests, hidden solutions, no GPU (graphics processing unit) needed.
