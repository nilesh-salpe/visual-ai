Multimodal LLMs: key papers

Every paper cited on the decks' Key papers slides, in the order the guide introduces them. Each entry links to the paper and to the decks that use it.

An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale
Dosovitskiy et al. · ICLR 2021 · cited in Vision encoders: ViT and CLIP

A pure transformer over sequences of image patches (16 × 16 pixels, linearly embedded, with a [class] token); pretrained on ImageNet-21k or JFT-300M it matches or beats CNNs with substantially less training compute.

Remember: 88.55% on ImageNet (best model)
Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision (CLIP)
Radford et al. · ICML 2021 · cited in Vision encoders: ViT and CLIP

Image and text encoders trained contrastively to predict which caption goes with which image, on 400 million internet pairs; text prompts enable zero-shot transfer to over 30 datasets.

Remember: matches the original ResNet-50 on ImageNet zero-shot, without its 1.28M training examples
Sigmoid Loss for Language Image Pre-Training (SigLIP)
Zhai et al. · ICCV 2023 · cited in Vision encoders: ViT and CLIP

A pairwise sigmoid loss that needs no batch-wide normalisation; works at small batch sizes and scales up; benefits of batch size diminish, 32k being sufficient.

Remember: 84.5% zero-shot ImageNet with 4 TPUv4 chips in two days (SigLiT)
Visual Instruction Tuning (LLaVA)
Liu et al. · NeurIPS 2023 · cited in Connecting vision to an LLM

Connects a CLIP vision encoder to an LLM through a projection and instruction-tunes on multimodal conversations generated by language-only GPT-4.

Remember: 85.1% relative score vs GPT-4 on a synthetic benchmark; 92.53% on ScienceQA with GPT-4
Improved Baselines with Visual Instruction Tuning (LLaVA-1.5)
Liu et al. · CVPR 2024 · cited in Connecting vision to an LLM

An MLP projector with CLIP-ViT-L-336px plus academic VQA (visual question answering) data with response-format prompts; state of the art on 11 benchmarks.

Remember: 1.2M public examples; about one day of training on a single 8-A100 node (13B)
Flamingo: a Visual Language Model for Few-Shot Learning
Alayrac et al. · NeurIPS 2022 · cited in Connecting vision to an LLM

Bridges frozen vision and language models with a Perceiver Resampler (a fixed 64 visual outputs from any image or video) and gated cross-attention layers; trained on interleaved image-text web data for in-context few-shot learning.

BLIP-2: Bootstrapping Language-Image Pre-training with Frozen Image Encoders and Large Language Models
Li et al. · ICML 2023 · cited in Connecting vision to an LLM

A lightweight Querying Transformer (32 learned queries) between a frozen image encoder and a frozen LLM, pretrained in two stages.

Remember: beats Flamingo-80B by 8.7% on zero-shot VQAv2 with 54× fewer trainable parameters
Chameleon: Mixed-Modal Early-Fusion Foundation Models
Chameleon Team (Meta) · arXiv 2024 · cited in Connecting vision to an LLM

Early-fusion, token-based model that understands and generates images and text in any order within one sequence.

Vision (Claude API)
Anthropic documentation · platform.claude.com 2026 · cited in Images as tokens in practice

Images cost ⌈width/28⌉ × ⌈height/28⌉ visual tokens; images above a tier limit are downscaled (standard 1,568 px / 1,568 tokens; high-resolution models 2,576 px / 4,784 tokens); up to 600 images per request (100 on 200k-context models) with stricter sizes above 20; images before text work best.

Robust Speech Recognition via Large-Scale Weak Supervision (Whisper)
Radford et al. · ICML 2023 · cited in Images as tokens in practice

Speech recognition trained on 680,000 hours of multilingual, multitask weakly supervised audio; generalises zero-shot and approaches human robustness.

MMMU: A Massive Multi-discipline Multimodal Understanding and Reasoning Benchmark for Expert AGI (artificial general intelligence)
Yue et al. · CVPR 2024 · cited in Images as tokens in practice

11.5K college-level multimodal questions across 6 disciplines, 30 subjects, 183 subfields and 30 image types (charts, maps, music sheets, chemical structures).

Remember: GPT-4V 56%, Gemini Ultra 59%
Evaluating Object Hallucination in Large Vision-Language Models (POPE)
Li et al. · EMNLP 2023 · cited in Images as tokens in practice

Vision-language models hallucinate objects, especially ones frequent in visual instructions or co-occurring with real objects; POPE evaluates this with polling yes/no questions.
