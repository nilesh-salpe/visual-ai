Slurm for GPU clusters

The workload manager behind most supercomputers and many AI (artificial intelligence) training clusters: daemons, partitions and QOS (quality of service), GPUs (graphics processing units) as GRES (generic resources), multifactor priority and fair share, backfill, topology-aware placement and preemption. Pinned to Slurm 26.05.

What is the problem?A lab needs to share thousands of GPUs between teams, start multi-node training jobs with all their GPUs at once, place them on nearby nodes, account for usage, and keep the cluster full.
Core ideaslurmctld holds the queue and decides; slurmd on every node launches work; slurmdbd stores accounting. Jobs (sbatch, srun) request nodes and GPUs (GRES); partitions group nodes like queues, and QOS sets limits and priority. The multifactor plugin ranks jobs, the backfill plugin fills gaps, topology plugins keep jobs under few switches, and preemption requeues or cancels lower-priority work.

1 · Problem

Many teams, one cluster

Allocation

Grant users exclusive or shared access to nodes for a period of time.

Launch

Start, run and monitor parallel work (one process per GPU) on the allocated nodes.

Arbitration

Queue pending work and settle contention for resources: Slurm's three stated functions.

NVIDIA acquired SchedMD, Slurm's main developer, in December 2025 and said Slurm stays open-source and vendor-neutral; NVIDIA reports Slurm runs on more than half of the top 10 and top 100 TOP500 systems.

2 · Core idea

Daemons, partitions, GRES, plugins

Daemons

slurmctld (central manager, optional backup), slurmd (one per compute node), slurmdbd (optional accounting database).

Partitions and QOS

Partitions are groups of nodes that act as job queues; QOS adds priority, limits and preemption rules.

GPUs as GRES

--gres=gpu:8 per node or --gpus per job (and --gpus-per-node / --gpus-per-task); Slurm sets CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES so concurrent jobs get separate GPUs.

Plugins

priority/multifactor (ranking), backfill (gap filling), topology/tree or /block (placement), preempt/qos or /partition_prio.

#!/bin/bash
#SBATCH --job-name=pretrain-7b
#SBATCH --partition=train          # a group of nodes acting as a queue
#SBATCH --nodes=16                 # 16 nodes × 8 GPUs = 128 GPUs, all at once
#SBATCH --gpus-per-node=8
#SBATCH --ntasks-per-node=8        # one process per GPU
#SBATCH --time=48:00:00            # the time limit backfill relies on
#SBATCH --requeue                  # may be requeued after preemption or node failure
#SBATCH --switches=1@01:00:00      # wait up to 1 h for nodes under one leaf switch

srun python train.py --resume-from latest

4 · Low-level design

How Slurm picks the next job

Preemption setting Meaning
PreemptType=preempt/qos or preempt/partition_prio who may preempt whom: QOS rules, or partition PriorityTier
PreemptMode=REQUEUE requeue the victim if it allows requeue (--requeue or JobRequeue=1), else cancel
PreemptMode=CANCEL / SUSPEND cancel; or suspend and resume later (SUSPEND needs GANG)
GraceTime SIGCONT and SIGTERM at once, SIGKILL when the grace time ends (default 0)
GANG trap: in Slurm, "gang scheduling" means time-slicing jobs in a partition, not all-at-once placement

5 · Trade-offs

Trade-offs

Choice Benefit Cost
Slurm mature HPC (high-performance computing) scheduler: gang allocation, backfill, topology, accounting static nodes; less native fit for services and containers
Exclusive nodes (default for training partitions) predictable performance idle GPUs for small jobs
GPU sharding (gres/shard) many small jobs per GPU no isolation between processes on the GPU
Fair Tree vs classic fair share fairer across account hierarchies harder to explain to users
--switches with a wait better collective bandwidth jobs wait longer; set max_switch_wait
6 · Failure modes

Failure modes

Failure Symptom Mitigation
Node with a bad GPU jobs crash on the same node again and again health checks drain it; nodes with fewer GPUs than configured are drained
Jobs without --requeue preempted or failed work is cancelled, not retried set --requeue or JobRequeue=1; checkpoint
Unrealistic time limits backfill reservations wrong enforce limits; per-partition defaults
slurmctld overload slow squeue, delayed scheduling limit bf_max_job_test and queue depth; job arrays instead of many jobs
Misread "GANG" time-slicing enabled instead of all-at-once placement Slurm allocates all of a job's nodes together by default
7 · Challenges in production

In production

Partitions per workload

Separate partitions or QOS for pretraining, research and interactive use, with preemption rules between them.

Prolog and epilog

Check GPUs and clean up before and after every job; drain nodes that fail.

Accounting

slurmdbd usage drives fair share and chargeback; review shares quarterly.

Topology config

Keep topology.conf (or topology.yaml) in sync with the real network.

Slurm Workload Manager overview
SchedMD · slurm.schedmd.com 2026

Three functions (allocate access to resources, launch and monitor work, arbitrate contention via a queue); slurmctld central manager with optional backup, slurmd on each node, optional slurmdbd accounting; nodes, partitions, jobs and job steps.

Generic Resource (GRES) Scheduling
SchedMD · slurm.schedmd.com 2026

GPUs as GRES (gres.conf, AutoDetect=nvml); --gres=gpu:N per node, --gpus per job, --gpus-per-node/-task; CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES set per step; MIG (Multi-Instance GPU, hardware partitioning of one GPU) instances as GPUs (pre-partitioned); gres/shard sharing without isolation; nodes with fewer resources than configured are drained.

Multifactor Priority Plugin
SchedMD · slurm.schedmd.com 2026

Priority = weighted sum of factors in [0, 1] (age, association, fair-share, job size, partition, QOS, site, TRES (trackable resources)) minus nice; every weight defaults to 0; PriorityDecayHalfLife defaults to 7 days; Fair Tree is the default fair-share algorithm since 19.05.

Classic Fairshare Algorithm
SchedMD · slurm.schedmd.com 2026

F = 2^(−U/S) (refined with effective usage UE through the account tree), with usage decayed by PriorityDecayHalfLife; still available with PriorityFlags=NO_FAIR_TREE.

Scheduling Configuration Guide (Slurm 26.05)
SchedMD · slurm.schedmd.com 2026

The main scheduler tests jobs in strict priority order per partition (sched_interval 60 s); backfill starts lower-priority jobs that do not delay higher-priority ones and needs realistic time limits (bf_interval 30 s, bf_window 1,440 min, bf_max_job_test 100, bf_resolution 60 s).

Topology Guide
SchedMD · slurm.schedmd.com 2026

topology/tree (lowest switch that fits, best-fit leaf switches; --switches=count@time), topology/block (fixed-size blocks to reduce fragmentation; --segment), topology/ring (26.05) and topology/torus3d.

Preemption
SchedMD · slurm.schedmd.com 2026

PreemptType none / partition_prio / qos; PreemptMode CANCEL, REQUEUE (only with --requeue or JobRequeue=1), SUSPEND (with GANG), GANG (time-slicing jobs in a partition), OFF; GraceTime sends SIGCONT and SIGTERM at once and SIGKILL at the new end time.
