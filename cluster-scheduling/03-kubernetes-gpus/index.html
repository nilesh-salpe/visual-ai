Kubernetes for GPU workloads

How Kubernetes exposes GPUs (device plugins, Dynamic Resource Allocation), why the default scheduler is not enough for training, and how Kueue and JobSet add queues, quotas, all-or-nothing admission and topology-aware placement. Kubernetes 1.35.

What is the problem?Kubernetes schedules one pod at a time, has no queue or fair share across teams, and knows nothing about network topology. A 128-GPU training job is 16 pods that must all start together on nearby nodes, or it deadlocks against another half-started job.
Core ideaExpose GPUs with a device plugin (nvidia.com/gpu, whole GPUs in limits) or DRA (Dynamic Resource Allocation) (stable in 1.35: claims, device classes, attribute filters, sharing). Put Kueue in front of the scheduler: LocalQueues feed ClusterQueues with quotas per ResourceFlavor, cohorts lend unused quota, workloads are admitted as a unit, waitForPodsReady evicts and requeues jobs that do not get all pods running, and topology-aware scheduling keeps them in one block or rack. JobSet runs a training job as one unit.

1 · Problem

Kubernetes was built for services

Pod-by-pod scheduling

The default scheduler places each pod independently; nothing ensures all 16 pods of a job start.

No queueing or quotas

Pending pods are not ordered by team share or priority across namespaces.

No topology

Pods can land in different racks or blocks, far apart on the network.

2 · Core idea

GPUs as resources, Kueue as the queue

Device plugin

The vendor plugin exposes nvidia.com/gpu; GPUs go in limits (requests default to the limit and must equal it).

DRA

Dynamic Resource Allocation, stable since 1.35: DeviceClasses, ResourceClaims and ResourceSlices; sharing between pods and expression-based (CEL (Common Expression Language)) device filtering, which device plugins lack.

Kueue

Decides when a job waits, is admitted to start, or is preempted; quotas per flavor; fair sharing; StrictFIFO or BestEffortFIFO (default).

JobSet

A group of Jobs managed as one unit (e.g. leader and workers); restarts the whole set on failure, exclusive placement per topology domain, a headless service for stable hostnames.

5 · Trade-offs

Trade-offs

Choice Benefit Cost
Kubernetes + Kueue one platform for training, inference and services; cloud-native tooling more moving parts than Slurm for pure batch
Device plugin simple, widely supported whole devices per container; no sharing or attribute filters
DRA claims, sharing, CEL filters, per-workload configuration newer; drivers must support it
BestEffortFIFO (default) small jobs pass blocked big ones big jobs can wait long
Borrowing in cohorts idle quota gets used borrowed capacity may be reclaimed by preemption
6 · Failure modes

Failure modes

Failure Symptom Mitigation
Partial placement deadlock jobs hold some GPUs and wait forever Kueue admission plus waitForPodsReady
Scattered pods slow all-reduce across racks topology-aware scheduling, JobSet exclusive placement
One failed pod the rest of the job hangs JobSet failure policy recreates the whole set; resume from checkpoint
Quota mismatch with real capacity admitted jobs cannot schedule keep quotas in line with nodes; waitForPodsReady as a backstop
Reclaimed borrowed quota jobs preempted unexpectedly checkpoint; run long jobs within nominal quota
7 · Challenges in production

In production

GPU stack

Drivers, device plugin or DRA driver, and node labels (Node Feature Discovery) on every GPU node.

Flavors per hardware

One ResourceFlavor per GPU type and capacity type (reserved, spot), with quotas per team.

Priorities and preemption

WorkloadPriorityClasses and ClusterQueue preemption policies (withinClusterQueue, reclaimWithinCohort) default to Never: set them deliberately.

Same cluster, many workloads

Kubernetes shines when training shares capacity with inference and services.

Further reading

Key papers

The primary sources behind this deck, each with what it contributed. Links go to the paper (arXiv or the conference page).

Schedule GPUs (Kubernetes)
Kubernetes documentation · kubernetes.io 2026

Vendor device plugins expose resources such as nvidia.com/gpu; GPUs are specified in limits (requests default to and must equal limits); node labels, selectors and Node Feature Discovery target GPU types.

Dynamic Resource Allocation (Kubernetes)
Kubernetes documentation · kubernetes.io 2026

Stable since v1.35 (first in v1.30): DeviceClasses, ResourceClaims and ResourceSlices; devices can be shared through a claim and filtered with CEL expressions, unlike device plugins with per-container requests.

Kueue overview
Kubernetes SIG (special interest group) Scheduling · kueue.sigs.k8s.io 2026

Kubernetes-native job queueing: decides when a job waits, is admitted or is preempted; quotas and fair sharing, resource flavors, admission checks, all-or-nothing scheduling with a timeout, topology-aware scheduling; integrates with Jobs, JobSet, Ray and Kubeflow Trainer.

Cluster Queue (Kueue)
Kubernetes SIG Scheduling · kueue.sigs.k8s.io 2026

Cluster-scoped quotas per ResourceFlavor (nominalQuota, borrowingLimit, lendingLimit); cohorts share unused quota; StrictFIFO or BestEffortFIFO (default); preemption withinClusterQueue and reclaimWithinCohort default to Never.

Setup All-or-nothing with ready Pods (waitForPodsReady)
Kubernetes SIG Scheduling · kueue.sigs.k8s.io 2026

Kueue watches an admitted workload until all its pods are ready; if not within the timeout (default 30 minutes) it is evicted and requeued with exponential backoff (60 s up to 3,600 s), which breaks partial-allocation deadlocks between distributed jobs.

Topology Aware Scheduling (Kueue)
Kubernetes SIG Scheduling · kueue.sigs.k8s.io 2026

Beta and on by default since Kueue 0.14: a Topology object over node labels (block, rack, node); podset-required-topology keeps all pods in one domain, podset-preferred-topology tries and moves up a level.

JobSet overview
Kubernetes SIG Apps · jobset.sigs.k8s.io 2026

A group of Kubernetes Jobs managed as a unit: separate templates per replicated job, the whole set recreated on failure per its failure policy, exclusive placement per topology domain, and a headless service for pod discovery.
