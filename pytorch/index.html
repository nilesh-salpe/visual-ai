PyTorch for LLMs

The PyTorch you need to build and train LLMs (large language models): tensors and views, autograd, nn.Module, the training loop, data pipelines, mixed precision, torch.compile and fused attention, and distributed training, ending with a tiny GPT. Checked against PyTorch 2.14.1; every notebook was executed with it before publishing.

🛠 Build it: 9 Colab notebooks

Split attention heads with strides, write a custom autograd Function, build a transformer block with SDPA (scaled dot-product attention), a resumable training loop, padding and packing collators, autocast and GradScaler, torch.compile graph-break hunting, DDP (distributed data parallel) with torchrun, and a tiny GPT, all runnable on a CPU (central processing unit) runtime.
