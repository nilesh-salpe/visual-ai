Attention Is All You Need: key papers

Every paper cited on the decks' Key papers slides, in the order the guide introduces them. Each entry links to the paper and to the decks that use it.

Attention Is All You Need
Vaswani et al. · NeurIPS 2017 · cited in Sequential bottleneck, Transformer architecture, Self-attention, Queries, keys, values, Scaled dot-product attention, Multi-head attention, Positional encoding, Encoder-decoder attention, Masked self-attention, Residuals + LayerNorm, Position-wise FFN, Training vs generation, Why it works, Experimental results

The Transformer: attention-only encoder-decoder with post-norm residual blocks, sinusoidal positions and a ReLU (rectified linear unit) FFN (feed-forward network). The baseline every later change is measured against.

Remember: 28.4 BLEU (Bilingual Evaluation Understudy, an n-gram overlap score for translation) on WMT 2014 English-German
Sequence to Sequence Learning with Neural Networks
Sutskever et al. · NeurIPS 2014 · cited in Sequential bottleneck, Masked self-attention, Training vs generation

A deep LSTM encodes the source into one fixed vector and another LSTM decodes it: the encoder-decoder the Transformer replaced.

Remember: 34.8 BLEU on WMT'14 English-French
Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate
Bahdanau et al. · ICLR 2015 · cited in Sequential bottleneck, Self-attention, Encoder-decoder attention

Argues a fixed-length sentence vector is the bottleneck of encoder-decoders and lets the decoder (soft-)search the source: the original attention mechanism.

Neural Machine Translation in Linear Time (ByteNet)
Kalchbrenner et al. · arXiv 2016 · cited in Transformer architecture, Experimental results

A dilated convolutional encoder-decoder that runs in time linear in sequence length; a parallel alternative to RNNs.

Convolutional Sequence to Sequence Learning (ConvS2S)
Gehring et al. · ICML 2017 · cited in Transformer architecture, Experimental results

A fully convolutional sequence-to-sequence model with gated linear units and per-layer attention, fully parallel in training.

Remember: beats the deep LSTM (long short-term memory network) of Wu et al. at an order of magnitude faster speed
Google's Neural Machine Translation System (GNMT)
Wu et al. · arXiv 2016 · cited in Transformer architecture, Encoder-decoder attention, Training vs generation, Experimental results

The production deep-LSTM translation system with attention and wordpieces that the Transformer was compared against.

Remember: 60% fewer translation errors than phrase-based production in side-by-side evaluation
Effective Approaches to Attention-based Neural Machine Translation
Luong et al. · EMNLP 2015 · cited in Self-attention, Queries, keys, values, Scaled dot-product attention

Compares global and local attention and simpler (dot-product) scoring functions for encoder-decoder translation.

Are Sixteen Heads Really Better than One?
Michel et al. · NeurIPS 2019 · cited in Multi-head attention, Why it works

Finds that a large percentage of attention heads can be removed at test time without much loss; some layers work with a single head.

Self-Attention with Relative Position Representations
Shaw et al. · NAACL 2018 · cited in Positional encoding, Why it works

Adds learned relative-distance representations inside self-attention instead of absolute position vectors at the input.

Remember: +1.3 BLEU (EN-DE) and +0.3 BLEU (EN-FR) over absolute positions
RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding
Su et al. · arXiv 2021 · cited in Positional encoding

Rotates query and key pairs by position-dependent angles so the attention score depends only on relative position, with a long-term decay property.

Remember: θᵢ = 10000^(−2i/d)
Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition (ResNet)
He et al. · CVPR 2016 · cited in Residuals + LayerNorm

Residual connections make very deep networks trainable by letting each block learn a correction to the identity.

Remember: 152 layers; 3.57% ImageNet test error (ensemble)
Layer Normalization
Ba et al. · arXiv 2016 · cited in Residuals + LayerNorm

Normalizes over all features of one training case instead of over the mini-batch, so it works for any batch size and for recurrent nets.

On Layer Normalization in the Transformer Architecture
Xiong et al. · ICML 2020 · cited in Residuals + LayerNorm

Mean-field analysis showing post-norm has large gradients near the output at initialization, so it needs learning-rate warm-up; pre-norm does not.

Remember: pre-LN trains without warm-up
GLU (gated linear unit) Variants Improve Transformer
Shazeer · arXiv 2020 · cited in Position-wise FFN

Replaces the Transformer FFN with gated linear units (ReGLU, GEGLU, SwiGLU…) at equal parameters by shrinking the hidden size to ⅔.

Remember: log-perplexity 1.677 (ReLU) → 1.636 (SwiGLU)
