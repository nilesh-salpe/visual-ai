Sequential processing bottleneck

RNNs (recurrent neural networks) process one step at a time, with O(n) paths between distant words. Self-attention needs O(1) of both.

Positional encoding

Attention ignores order. Sinusoids at many frequencies fix that, and offsets become rotations.
