LLM Inference and Serving: key papers

Every paper cited on the decks' Key papers slides, in the order the guide introduces them. Each entry links to the paper and to the decks that use it.

Efficiently Scaling Transformer Inference
Pope et al. · MLSys 2023 · cited in The inference workload, KV cache, Parallelism for inference

An analytical model of inference cost that picks how to partition a model across chips for a given latency target; shows prefill and decode need different strategies and that multi-query attention allows much longer contexts.

Remember: 29 ms/token on PaLM 540B with int8 weights; 76% MFU (model floating-point-operation utilization, the share of peak compute spent on the model's math) on large-batch prefill; MQA enables up to 32× longer context
DistServe: Disaggregating Prefill and Decoding for Goodput-optimized Large Language Model Serving
Zhong et al. · OSDI 2024 · cited in The inference workload, Disaggregated serving, Putting it together

Defines serving quality as goodput, the request rate served within both TTFT and TPOT targets, and runs prefill and decode on separate GPUs so they stop interfering.

Remember: 7.4× more requests or 12.6× tighter SLO than colocated systems
Splitwise: Efficient Generative LLM Inference Using Phase Splitting
Patel et al. · ISCA 2024 · cited in The inference workload, Disaggregated serving

Characterizes prompt computation as compute-intensive and token generation as memory-intensive, and splits them onto separate machines, possibly of different GPU types.

Remember: 1.4× throughput at 20% lower cost, or 2.35× throughput at the same cost and power
Fast Transformer Decoding: One Write-Head is All You Need
Shazeer · arXiv 2019 · cited in KV cache

Introduces multi-query attention: all heads share one key/value head, cutting the memory bandwidth of incremental decoding with only minor quality loss.

Remember: 1 K/V head instead of h
GQA: Training Generalized Multi-Query Transformer Models from Multi-Head Checkpoints
Ainslie et al. · EMNLP 2023 · cited in KV cache

Grouped-query attention: an intermediate number of key/value heads, plus a recipe to convert existing multi-head checkpoints.

Remember: quality close to MHA, speed close to MQA; uptraining with 5% of the original pretraining compute
DeepSeek-V2: A Strong, Economical, and Efficient Mixture-of-Experts Language Model
DeepSeek-AI · arXiv 2024 · cited in KV cache

Introduces multi-head latent attention (MLA), which compresses keys and values into a small latent vector per token, alongside a Mixture-of-Experts design.

Remember: KV cache reduced by 93.3%; maximum generation throughput 5.76× vs DeepSeek 67B
Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention
Kwon et al. · SOSP 2023 · cited in PagedAttention and prefix caching, Batching, Putting it together

Stores the KV cache in fixed-size blocks mapped by a block table, like virtual memory pages, giving near-zero fragmentation and sharing with copy-on-write. The basis of vLLM.

Remember: 2–4× throughput vs FasterTransformer and Orca at the same latency
SGLang: Efficient Execution of Structured Language Model Programs
Zheng et al. · NeurIPS 2024 · cited in PagedAttention and prefix caching, Putting it together

A runtime and frontend for multi-call LLM programs; RadixAttention keeps KV caches in a radix tree so shared prefixes are reused across requests automatically.

Remember: up to 6.4× higher throughput
Orca: A Distributed Serving System for Transformer-Based Generative Models
Yu et al. · OSDI 2022 · cited in Batching

Iteration-level scheduling (requests join and leave the batch after every decode step) plus selective batching (batch the matmuls, run attention per request).

Remember: 36.9× throughput over FasterTransformer at the same latency (GPT-3 175B)
SARATHI: Efficient LLM (large language model) Inference by Piggybacking Decodes with Chunked Prefills
Agrawal et al. · arXiv 2023 · cited in Chunked prefill

Splits prefills into equal chunks and fills the rest of each batch with decodes, which "piggyback" on the compute-heavy chunk.

Remember: up to 10× decode throughput (LLaMA-13B, A6000)
Taming Throughput-Latency Tradeoff in LLM Inference with Sarathi-Serve
Agrawal et al. · OSDI 2024 · cited in Chunked prefill, Putting it together

Chunked prefills plus stall-free scheduling: new requests are added without pausing ongoing decodes, so throughput rises without TPOT spikes.

Remember: 2.6× serving capacity (Mistral-7B, 1 A100) and up to 3.7× (Yi-34B, 2 A100s) vs vLLM; up to 5.6× with pipeline parallelism (Falcon-180B)
Fast Inference from Transformers via Speculative Decoding
Leviathan et al. · ICML 2023 · cited in Speculative decoding

A small model drafts tokens, the large model verifies them in parallel, and a modified rejection rule keeps the output distribution exactly unchanged.

Remember: 2–3× faster on T5-XXL with identical outputs
Accelerating Large Language Model Decoding with Speculative Sampling
Chen et al. · arXiv 2023 · cited in Speculative decoding

Independent derivation of speculative sampling with a modified rejection scheme that preserves the target distribution.

Remember: 2–2.5× decoding speedup on Chinchilla 70B
LLM.int8(): 8-bit Matrix Multiplication for Transformers at Scale
Dettmers et al. · NeurIPS 2022 · cited in Quantization

Finds systematic outlier features in large transformers and handles them with a mixed-precision decomposition: outlier dimensions in 16-bit, everything else in 8-bit.

Remember: more than 99.9% of values multiplied in 8-bit, no degradation up to 175B
SmoothQuant: Accurate and Efficient Post-Training Quantization for Large Language Models
Xiao et al. · ICML 2023 · cited in Quantization

Migrates quantization difficulty from activation outliers to weights with a mathematically equivalent per-channel scaling, enabling W8A8 (8-bit weights and activations).

Remember: up to 1.56× speedup and 2× memory reduction
GPTQ: Accurate Post-Training Quantization for Generative Pre-trained Transformers
Frantar et al. · ICLR 2023 · cited in Quantization

One-shot weight quantization using approximate second-order information, quantizing one column at a time and compensating the error in the rest.

Remember: 175B model to 3–4 bits in about four GPU hours
AWQ: Activation-aware Weight Quantization for LLM Compression and Acceleration
Lin et al. · MLSys 2024 · cited in Quantization

Weight-only low-bit quantization that protects the most important weight channels, chosen by activation magnitude, by scaling them up before quantizing.

Remember: protecting 1% of salient weights greatly reduces error; >3× over Hugging Face FP16 with its TinyChat kernels
FP8 Formats for Deep Learning
Micikevicius et al. · arXiv 2022 · cited in Quantization

Defines the two FP8 encodings, E4M3 (more precision) and E5M2 (more range), and shows FP8 training and post-training quantization matching 16-bit results.

Remember: E4M3 max 448, E5M2 max 57,344
Megatron-LM: Training Multi-Billion Parameter Language Models Using Model Parallelism
Shoeybi et al. · arXiv 2019 · cited in Parallelism for inference

Intra-layer (tensor) model parallelism that splits each attention and MLP block across GPUs with only a few all-reduce operations, implemented in plain PyTorch.

Remember: 2 all-reduces per transformer block in the forward pass; 76% scaling efficiency on 512 GPUs
