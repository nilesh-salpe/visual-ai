LLM Inference and Serving

How production systems serve large language models fast and cheaply: the prefill/decode split, the KV (key-value) cache, batching and scheduling, memory management, speculative decoding, quantization, parallelism and disaggregation, then serving at scale: large MoE (Mixture of Experts) models across many GPUs (graphics processing units) and the KV cache across a fleet. Part 1 decks follow the 9-beat template, Part 2 decks are answer-first; figures are checked against the source papers, and most concepts have a hands-on Colab notebook.

🛠 Build it: 13 Colab notebooks

Implement a KV cache, a continuous-batching scheduler, a paged allocator, speculative sampling, SmoothQuant, FP8 (8-bit floating point) rounding, tensor-parallel layers and the sampling pipeline (temperature, top-k, top-p, min-p, beam search) in NumPy. Built-in tests, hidden solutions, no GPU (graphics processing unit) needed.

Part 1 · One server

01

The inference workload

Prefill vs decode, TTFT (time to first token), TPOT (time per output token) and goodput under SLOs (service-level objectives).

11

Decoding strategies

Greedy, beam search, temperature, top-k, top-p, min-p, penalties; why T = 0 is not deterministic.

02

KV cache

Exact caching of K and V; memory maths; GQA (grouped-query attention), MQA (multi-query attention) and MLA (multi-head latent attention).

05

PagedAttention and prefix caching

Blocks, block tables, copy-on-write; RadixAttention prefix reuse.

03

Continuous batching

Iteration-level scheduling (Orca): no idle slots.

04

Chunked prefill

Token budgets: decodes first, prefill chunks after (Sarathi-Serve).

06

Speculative decoding

Draft, verify, exact rejection sampling; speedup maths.

07

Quantization

Scales, outliers, SmoothQuant, GPTQ/AWQ (Activation-aware Weight Quantization), FP8.

08

Parallelism for inference

Megatron TP (tensor parallelism), two all-reduces per layer, why it stays on NVLink.

09

Disaggregated serving

Separate prefill and decode pools, KV transfer (DistServe, Splitwise).

Decks in the answer-first format: short answer, a picture to draw, an animation, numbers, trade-offs, follow-up questions and practice.

Serving large MoE models

The KV cache across the fleet

CPU (central processing unit), SSD (solid-state drive) and shared tiers; load vs recompute; prefix-aware routing; KV transfer; context parallelism.

Putting it together

Symptom → cause → fix playbook; how engines combine the pieces.
