Agent context: key papers

Every paper cited on the decks' Key papers slides, in the order the guide introduces them. Each entry links to the paper and to the decks that use it.

Effective context engineering for AI (artificial intelligence) agents
Anthropic · Anthropic Engineering 2025 · cited in Memory and context management

Context as a finite "attention budget" (context rot); just-in-time retrieval; compaction, structured note-taking and sub-agents for long-horizon tasks.

MemGPT: Towards LLMs (large language models) as Operating Systems
Packer et al. · arXiv 2023 · cited in Memory and context management

Virtual context management: the LLM moves information between a limited main context and external memory tiers via function calls, like OS paging.

Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior
Park et al. · UIST 2023 · cited in Memory and context management

A memory stream of observations retrieved by recency, importance and relevance, periodically synthesised into reflections that guide planning.

Remember: retrieval score = recency + importance + relevance (each min-max scaled, weights 1)
Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts
Liu et al. · TACL 2024 · cited in Memory and context management, RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Performance is often highest when relevant information is at the beginning or end of the context and degrades when it is in the middle.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP (natural language processing) Tasks
Lewis et al. · NeurIPS 2020 · cited in Agentic retrieval (RAG), RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Combines a seq2seq generator with a dense retriever over Wikipedia (non-parametric memory), trained end to end.

Self-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection
Asai et al. · ICLR 2024 · cited in Agentic retrieval (RAG)

Trains the model to decide when to retrieve and to critique retrieved passages and its own output with reflection tokens.

Corrective Retrieval Augmented Generation
Yan et al. · arXiv 2024 · cited in Agentic retrieval (RAG)

A lightweight evaluator grades retrieved documents and triggers corrective actions (refinement or web search) when retrieval is poor.

Not what you've signed up for: Compromising Real-World LLM-Integrated (LLM = large language model) Applications with Indirect Prompt Injection
Greshake et al. · AISec 2023 · cited in Agentic retrieval (RAG)

Instructions planted in data the application retrieves (web pages, emails, documents) can hijack LLM-integrated apps: data theft, worming, manipulation.

Model Context Protocol specification (revision 2026-07-28)
MCP maintainers · modelcontextprotocol.io 2026 · cited in Model Context Protocol (MCP), Agent2Agent protocol (A2A)

Hosts, clients and servers over JSON-RPC 2.0; servers offer tools, resources and prompts; stdio and Streamable HTTP transports; stateless requests with per-request protocol version and capabilities.

MCP specification changelog (2026-07-28)
MCP maintainers · modelcontextprotocol.io 2026 · cited in Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Removes the initialize handshake and sessions, adds server/discover and subscriptions/listen, replaces server-initiated requests with multi round-trip requests (input_required).

Introducing the Model Context Protocol
Anthropic · Anthropic 2024 · cited in Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Announcement of MCP (November 25, 2024) as a universal, open standard for connecting AI systems to data sources and tools.

Agent2Agent (A2A) Protocol Specification (v1.0)
A2A Project · a2a-protocol.org 2026 · cited in Agent2Agent protocol (A2A)

Agent Cards at /.well-known/agent-card.json; Messages of Parts (exactly one of text, raw, url or data); Tasks with a lifecycle (SUBMITTED, WORKING, INPUT_REQUIRED, AUTH_REQUIRED, COMPLETED, FAILED, CANCELED, REJECTED) producing Artifacts; JSON-RPC, gRPC and HTTP+JSON bindings; streaming over SSE and push notifications. v1.0.0 released 12 March 2026.

Announcing the Agent2Agent Protocol (A2A)
Google · Google for Developers Blog 2025 · cited in Agent2Agent protocol (A2A)

An open protocol for agent-to-agent communication that complements MCP; built on HTTP, SSE and JSON-RPC; secure by default; long-running tasks; modality agnostic.

Remember: announced 9 April 2025 with more than 50 technology partners
A2A protocol surpasses 150 organizations, lands in major cloud platforms
Linux Foundation · Linux Foundation press release 2026 · cited in Agent2Agent protocol (A2A)

One-year milestone: version 1.0 is the first stable specification; integrations in Azure AI Foundry, Copilot Studio and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Runtime.

Remember: more than 150 supporting organizations (April 2026)
Fine-Tuning or Retrieval? Comparing Knowledge Injection in LLMs (large language models)
Ovadia, Brief, Mishaeli, Elisha · arXiv (Microsoft) 2023 · cited in RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Retrieval consistently outperformed unsupervised fine-tuning for injecting both existing and new knowledge; training on many paraphrases of a fact helps fine-tuning.

Remember: current events, Mistral 7B: 0.481 base, 0.504 fine-tuned, 0.588 with paraphrases, 0.875 with RAG
Does Fine-Tuning LLMs on New Knowledge Encourage Hallucinations?
Gekhman et al. · EMNLP 2024 · cited in RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Fine-tuning examples with knowledge the model lacks are learned much more slowly than known ones and, once learned, linearly increase hallucination; early stopping mitigates it.

Remember: models acquire new facts through pretraining, mostly not fine-tuning
Retrieval Augmented Generation or Long-Context LLMs? A Comprehensive Study and Hybrid Approach
Li et al. · EMNLP Industry 2024 · cited in RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Long context beats RAG on average when resourced sufficiently, but RAG is far cheaper; Self-Route lets the model declare a query unanswerable from retrieved chunks and only then uses long context.

Remember: identical predictions on over 60% of queries; cost −65% (Gemini-1.5-Pro), −39% (GPT-4o)
RULER: What's the Real Context Size of Your Long-Context Language Models?
Hsieh et al. · COLM 2024 · cited in RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Synthetic long-context benchmark beyond needle-in-a-haystack (multi-key retrieval, multi-hop tracing, aggregation); models degrade sharply as length grows.

Remember: 17 models; only about half of those claiming 32K+ perform satisfactorily at 32K
RAFT: Adapting Language Model to Domain Specific RAG
Zhang et al. · arXiv 2024 · cited in RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Fine-tune with questions, a golden document and distractor documents (and some examples with no golden document), with chain-of-thought answers that quote the source.

Remember: gains on PubMed, HotpotQA and Gorilla in-domain RAG
Introducing Contextual Retrieval
Anthropic · Anthropic News 2024 · cited in RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Prepend 50-100 tokens of chunk-specific context (written by an LLM from the whole document) before embedding and BM25 (Okapi Best Matching 25 ranking function) indexing; retrieve top 150, rerank, keep 20. Knowledge bases under 200,000 tokens can go straight into the prompt.

Remember: top-20 retrieval failures 5.7% → 3.7% (−35%), 2.9% with BM25 (−49%), 1.9% with reranking (−67%); $1.02 per million document tokens
Prompt caching
Anthropic documentation · platform.claude.com 2026 · cited in RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Reuse of cached prompt prefixes: 5-minute cache writes cost 1.25× base input, 1-hour writes 2×, and reads 0.1× on most models (lower on some newer ones).

Context windows (Claude documentation)
Anthropic · Claude platform docs 2026 · cited in RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Context window up to 1M tokens depending on the model; more context is not automatically better, because accuracy and recall degrade as token count grows (context rot).

Remember: up to 1M tokens; 200k on older models
LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models
Hu et al. · ICLR 2022 · cited in RAG, fine-tuning or long context

Freezes the pretrained weights and trains a low-rank update BA (B initialized to zero) that can be merged after training.

Remember: 10,000× fewer trainable parameters, 3× less GPU (graphics processing unit) memory (GPT-3 175B)
