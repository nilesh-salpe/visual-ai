Interpretability: key papers

Every paper cited on the decks' Key papers slides, in the order the guide introduces them. Each entry links to the paper and to the decks that use it.

Toy Models of Superposition
Elhage et al. (Anthropic) · Transformer Circuits Thread 2022 · cited in Features and superposition

Polysemanticity arises when models store more sparse features than dimensions in superposition; with dense features a toy model keeps only the most important (like PCA), with sparse features it packs more (e.g. five in two dimensions), with phase changes, polytope geometry and a link to adversarial examples.

In-context Learning and Induction Heads
Olsson et al. (Anthropic) · Transformer Circuits Thread 2022 · cited in Features and superposition, Circuits, probes and steering

Induction heads complete [A][B] ... [A] → [B]; they form at the same point as a sharp rise in in-context learning (a bump in the training loss); causal evidence in small attention-only models, correlational in larger ones.

Towards Monosemanticity: Decomposing Language Models With Dictionary Learning
Bricken et al. (Anthropic) · Transformer Circuits Thread 2023 · cited in Features and superposition, Sparse autoencoders

Sparse autoencoders on the 512-neuron MLP (multilayer perceptron) of a one-layer transformer, trained on 8 billion activations, with 512 to 131,072 features (analysis on 4,096); features for Arabic script, DNA, base64 and Hebrew; a single neuron mixes academic citations, English dialogue, HTTP requests and Korean text.

Sparse Autoencoders Find Highly Interpretable Features in Language Models
Cunningham et al. · ICLR 2024 · cited in Sparse autoencoders

SAE features are more interpretable and monosemantic than alternative decompositions, and pinpoint the features causally responsible for indirect-object identification more finely.

Scaling Monosemanticity: Extracting Interpretable Features from Claude 3 Sonnet
Templeton et al. (Anthropic) · Transformer Circuits Thread 2024 · cited in Sparse autoencoders

SAEs on the middle-layer residual stream of Claude 3 Sonnet with ~1M, ~4M and ~34M features; fewer than 300 active per token, at least 65% of variance explained; multilingual and multimodal features; safety-relevant features (code backdoors, bias, deception, power-seeking, sycophancy); feature clamping steers behaviour.

Remember: dead features ≈2% (1M), 35% (4M), 65% (34M)
Golden Gate Claude
Anthropic · Anthropic News 2024 · cited in Sparse autoencoders

Amplifying the Golden Gate Bridge feature in Claude 3 Sonnet made most replies mention the bridge; available as a 24-hour research demo.

Scaling and evaluating sparse autoencoders
Gao et al. (OpenAI) · arXiv 2024 · cited in Sparse autoencoders

k-sparse (top-k) autoencoders control sparsity directly and, with other changes, leave few dead latents; clean scaling laws in size and sparsity; new feature-quality metrics.

Remember: a 16 million latent SAE on GPT-4 activations, trained on 40 billion tokens
Gemma Scope: Open Sparse Autoencoders Everywhere All At Once on Gemma 2
Lieberum et al. (Google DeepMind) · arXiv 2024 · cited in Sparse autoencoders

Open JumpReLU SAEs trained on all layers and sub-layers of Gemma 2 2B and 9B (and select layers of 27B).

Interpretability in the Wild: a Circuit for Indirect Object Identification in GPT-2 small
Wang et al. · ICLR 2023 · cited in Circuits, probes and steering

A circuit of 26 attention heads in 7 classes, found with causal interventions and evaluated for faithfulness, completeness and minimality.

Locating and Editing Factual Associations in GPT
Meng et al. · NeurIPS 2022 · cited in Circuits, probes and steering

Causal tracing finds factual recall in mid-layer feed-forward modules at subject tokens; Rank-One Model Editing (ROME) updates a fact by modifying those weights.

On the Biology of a Large Language Model
Lindsey et al. (Anthropic) · Transformer Circuits Thread 2025 · cited in Circuits, probes and steering

Attribution graphs on a replacement model of Claude 3.5 Haiku: multi-hop reasoning (Dallas → Texas → Austin) alongside shortcuts, planning rhymes ahead, shared multilingual circuits; satisfying insight for about a quarter of prompts tried.

Steering Language Models With Activation Engineering
Turner et al. · arXiv 2023 · cited in Circuits, probes and steering

Activation Addition: the activation difference between contrasting prompts (e.g. "Love" − "Hate") added during the forward pass steers outputs; state-of-the-art sentiment shift and detoxification on LLaMA-3 and OPT with no optimisation.

Representation Engineering: A Top-Down Approach to AI (artificial intelligence) Transparency
Zou et al. · arXiv 2023 · cited in Circuits, probes and steering

Reads and controls population-level representations (honesty, harmlessness, power-seeking) rather than neurons or circuits.
