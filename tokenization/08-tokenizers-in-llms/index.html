Tokenizers in real LLMs

What GPT-2, GPT-4, GPT-4o, Llama, BERT and T5 actually use: algorithm, vocabulary size, pre-tokenization rules and special tokens. Every row is checked against the model's paper or the tokenizer source.

2 · Limitation

You can't swap a tokenizer after training

Ids index the embeddings

Id 1234 is a specific learned row in this model's embedding matrix. In another tokenizer, id 1234 is a different string.

Rules are baked in

How digits, spaces and code are split determines what patterns the model learned to read.

Special tokens are reserved

End-of-text, padding and chat-role markers are specific ids the model was trained to treat specially.

4 · Core idea

What a tokenizer actually consists of

Vocabulary

BPE merge ranks (tiktoken: about 100K–200K byte sequences with ranks), or scored pieces (a SentencePiece unigram model).

Pre-tokenization

A regex or rule that splits text into chunks before subword merging: words with their leading space, digit groups, punctuation.

Normalization

Optional Unicode normalization (SentencePiece defaults to NFKC). Byte-level BPE keeps raw bytes.

Special tokens

Reserved ids for document boundaries, padding, fill-in-the-middle or chat structure, never produced from ordinary text.

6 · Why it works

Why tokenizers differ

Different data

Tokenizers are trained on their model's data mix. More code and multilingual text in training produces tokens for those.

Different goals

Splitting digits individually (Llama) or into groups of three (cl100k/o200k) gives numbers a consistent structure. GPT-2's unbounded digit chunks don't.

Different eras

Vocabularies grew from about 30K (BERT, T5, Llama 2) to 100K–200K (GPT-4, Llama 3, GPT-4o) as models grew and multilingual use mattered more.

Tooling

SentencePiece (raw text, ▁) and tiktoken (byte-level BPE, regex pre-split) are the two families most LLMs build on.

7 · Misconceptions

Common misconceptions

Each statement below is a mental model people often get wrong. Decide why it is wrong, then reveal.

"Token ids are universal across models."

They're model-specific. The same id maps to different strings in different tokenizers, and even shared merges get different id assignments.

"Typing "<|endoftext|>" in a prompt ends the document."

Special tokens are reserved ids. With tiktoken's defaults the literal string raises an error instead of being encoded as the control token.

"o200k_base is cl100k_base with more tokens added."

It is a separate encoding: its own merge ranks and its own pre-tokenization pattern (tiktoken source).

9 · Key takeaway

Key takeaway

Each LLM's tokenizer (vocabulary, pre-tokenization, normalization and special tokens) is part of the model. Ids, counts and number handling differ between models and don't transfer.
Say it in 30 seconds

GPT-2 uses byte-level BPE with 50,257 tokens; GPT-4's cl100k_base and GPT-4o's o200k_base are larger byte-level BPE encodings that split numbers into groups of up to three digits. Llama 1 and 2 use SentencePiece BPE with 32K tokens and individual digits; Llama 3 uses 128K tokens (100K from tiktoken plus 28K multilingual). BERT uses 30,000 WordPieces and T5 32,000 SentencePiece pieces. Special tokens are reserved ids, and ids only make sense with the model's own tokenizer.

Problem: tokenizers differ per model, so counts, costs and ids don't transfer.
