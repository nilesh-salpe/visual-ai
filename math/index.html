Math for LLMs and neural networks

The mathematics every LLM (large language model) concept rests on, each idea tied to where it appears in a real model: vectors and matrices, the SVD (singular value decomposition) and low rank, derivatives and backpropagation, probability and sampling, maximum likelihood, information theory, softmax numerics, optimization and floating point. Every deck follows the 9-beat template and has an interactive widget.

🛠 Build it: 6 Colab notebooks

Implement cosine search, truncated SVD and LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) counts, hand-written gradients with a gradient checker, entropy/KL (Kullback-Leibler divergence)/perplexity, a stable and online softmax, and gradient descent, momentum and Adam in NumPy.
