📚 Reading list🔤 Glossary
01

Why training must be distributed

The memory and time walls; the four axes of parallelism.

02

Data parallelism

Ring all-reduce (≈ 2·S per GPU) and bucketed overlap.

03

ZeRO and FSDP

Shard optimizer state, gradients and parameters: 4×, 8×, N×.

04

Tensor and sequence parallelism

Megatron column/row splits, sequence parallelism, selective recompute.

05

Pipeline parallelism

GPipe, 1F1B, interleaved and zero-bubble schedules; the (p−1)/m bubble.

06

Context parallelism

Ring attention, all-gather K/V (Llama 3) and Ulysses all-to-all.

07

Expert parallelism

All-to-all dispatch and combine; DeepSeek-V3 at 64-way EP (expert parallelism).

08

Activation memory and recomputation

sbh(34 + 5as/h), √n checkpointing, selective recompute.

09

Mixed precision and FP8 training

FP32 (32-bit floating point) master weights, loss scaling, BF16 (bfloat16, a 16-bit floating-point format with the exponent range of 32-bit floats), fine-grained FP8 scales.

10

Putting it together: 4D layouts

TP (tensor parallelism) ⊂ CP (context parallelism) ⊂ PP (pipeline parallelism) ⊂ DP (data parallelism); Llama 3 405B on 16,384 GPUs.

11

Reliability and stability

Checkpoint intervals, stragglers, loss spikes, z-loss and QK-norm.
