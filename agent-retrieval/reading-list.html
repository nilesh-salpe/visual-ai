Retrieval stack: key papers

Every paper cited on the decks' Key papers slides, in the order the guide introduces them. Each entry links to the paper and to the decks that use it.

Introducing Contextual Retrieval
Anthropic · Anthropic News 2024 · cited in Indexing pipelines, Rerankers

Prepend 50-100 tokens of chunk-specific context (written by an LLM from the whole document) before embedding and BM25 indexing; retrieve top 150, rerank, keep 20. Knowledge bases under 200,000 tokens can go straight into the prompt.

Remember: top-20 retrieval failures 5.7% → 3.7% (−35%), 2.9% with BM25 (−49%), 1.9% with reranking (−67%); $1.02 per million document tokens
Late Chunking: Contextual Chunk Embeddings Using Long-Context Embedding Models
Günther et al. · arXiv 2024 · cited in Indexing pipelines

Embed the whole long text first and pool token embeddings per chunk afterwards, so each chunk embedding carries the surrounding context; no extra training needed.

BEIR: A Heterogenous Benchmark for Zero-shot Evaluation of Information Retrieval Models
Thakur et al. · NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks 2021 · cited in Indexing pipelines

18 retrieval datasets for zero-shot evaluation; BM25 is a robust baseline, and re-ranking and late-interaction models score best on average at higher compute cost.

MTEB: Massive Text Embedding Benchmark
Muennighoff et al. · EACL 2023 · cited in Indexing pipelines

8 embedding tasks, 58 datasets, 112 languages; no single embedding method dominates across all tasks.

Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering
Karpukhin et al. · EMNLP 2020 · cited in Indexing pipelines

A dual encoder (separate query and passage encoders) trained on question-passage pairs; retrieval by inner product.

Remember: +9 to 19 points top-20 accuracy over Lucene BM25
Efficient and robust approximate nearest neighbor search using Hierarchical Navigable Small World graphs
Malkov & Yashunin · IEEE TPAMI 2018 · cited in Vector databases

A multi-layer proximity graph: each element's top layer is drawn from an exponentially decaying distribution (mL = 1/ln M), search descends greedily from the top layer, and layer 0 keeps up to 2M links per element; logarithmic complexity scaling.

Product Quantization for Nearest Neighbor Search
Jégou, Douze & Schmid · IEEE TPAMI 2011 · cited in Vector databases

Split each vector into m subvectors and quantize each with its own small codebook, so a vector is stored as m short codes; distances are estimated from per-query lookup tables (asymmetric distance computation), combined with an inverted file (IVFADC).

DiskANN: Fast Accurate Billion-point Nearest Neighbor Search on a Single Node
Subramanya et al. · NeurIPS 2019 · cited in Vector databases

A graph index (Vamana) stored on SSD with compressed vectors in RAM serves a billion-point index from one workstation.

Remember: 1B points, 64 GB RAM, >5,000 queries/s, <3 ms mean latency, 95%+ 1-recall@1 on 16 cores
The Faiss library
Douze et al. · arXiv 2024 · cited in Vector databases

The vector-search toolkit behind many vector databases: flat, IVF, PQ and graph indexes, and the trade-off space between accuracy, speed, memory and build time.

Accelerating Large-Scale Inference with Anisotropic Vector Quantization
Guo et al. · ICML 2020 · cited in Vector databases

ScaNN: a quantization loss that penalises the error parallel to the data point more than the orthogonal error, which suits maximum inner product search.

Sentence-BERT: Sentence Embeddings using Siamese BERT-Networks
Reimers & Gurevych · EMNLP 2019 · cited in Rerankers

A bi-encoder producing one embedding per sentence, compared by cosine similarity.

Remember: most similar pair among 10,000 sentences takes ~65 h with a BERT cross-encoder vs ~5 s with SBERT
Passage Re-ranking with BERT
Nogueira & Cho · arXiv 2019 · cited in Rerankers

A BERT cross-encoder reads query and passage together and outputs a relevance score; used to rerank BM25 candidates.

Remember: +27% relative MRR@10 over the previous state of the art on MS MARCO
ColBERT: Efficient and Effective Passage Search via Contextualized Late Interaction over BERT
Khattab & Zaharia · SIGIR 2020 · cited in Rerankers

Late interaction: query and document are encoded separately into token embeddings (documents offline), then scored by summing each query token's maximum similarity (MaxSim).

Remember: competitive with BERT rerankers, two orders of magnitude faster, four orders fewer FLOPs (floating-point operations) per query
Is ChatGPT Good at Search? Investigating Large Language Models as Re-Ranking Agents
Sun et al. · EMNLP 2023 · cited in Rerankers

Listwise reranking by an instructed LLM that outputs a permutation, applied with a sliding window over BM25's top 100; the ranking ability distils into small models.

Remember: a distilled 440M model beats a 3B supervised reranker on BEIR
Reciprocal Rank Fusion outperforms Condorcet and individual Rank Learning Methods
Cormack, Clarke & Büttcher · SIGIR 2009 · cited in Rerankers

Fuse several rankings by score(d) = Σ 1/(k + rank_r(d)) with k = 60; simple, training-free and better than the individual rankings.

Rerank overview
Cohere documentation · docs.cohere.com 2026 · cited in Rerankers

A hosted reranker API that orders documents by relevance to a query and returns a relevance score per document; structured documents are best passed as YAML strings.
