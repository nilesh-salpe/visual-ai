📚 Reading list🔤 Glossary
01

What an eval measures

A score is a protocol: prompts, decoding, scoring, uncertainty. HELM (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models)'s 7 metrics.

02

Benchmarks and their failure modes

MMLU (Massive Multitask Language Understanding), GPQA (Graduate-level Google-Proof Q&A benchmark), GSM8K (Grade School Math 8K), HumanEval, SWE-bench (SWE = software engineering); floors, ceilings, saturation.

03

pass@k and sampling metrics

The unbiased estimator and choosing k.

04

Error bars and comparing models

Standard errors, clustered errors, paired tests, power analysis.

05

Contamination

13-gram overlap, clean vs dirty scores, fresh test sets.

06

LLM-as-judge

Agreement with humans; position, verbosity and self-enhancement bias.

07

Arenas and ratings

Bradley-Terry vs online Elo, confidence intervals.

08

Experiments in LLM training

Seed noise, low-noise metrics, small-to-large decisions, search.

Sources: Liang et al. 2022 (HELM); Sclar et al. 2023; Hendrycks et al. 2021 (MMLU); Wang et al. 2024 (MMLU-Pro); Rein et al. 2023 (GPQA); Cobbe et al. 2021 (GSM8K); Chen et al. 2021 (HumanEval, pass@k); Jimenez et al. 2024 (SWE-bench); Miller 2024 (error bars); Brown et al. 2020 (GPT-3 contamination); Zhang et al. 2024 (GSM1k); Zheng et al. 2023 (MT-Bench); Dubois et al. 2024 (length-controlled AlpacaEval); Chiang et al. 2024 (Chatbot Arena).
